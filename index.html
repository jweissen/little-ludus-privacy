Privacy Policy for Little Ludus

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Little Ludus ("the game", "the app") is a single-player game made by Flat Bunny Labs ("we", "us"). This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. We do not know who you are or how you play.

  • No account. You do not sign up or log in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, from us or from anyone else.
  • No purchases and no payment information.
  • No internet connection is used. The game works fully offline and the released app does not ask for permission to use the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (your ludus, residents, denarii, and settings such as sound and the speed option) is saved only on your own device, in the game's private storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to copy a save code from the menu and paste it somewhere yourself (for example to move your game to another device). That is your own copy, under your control. Uninstalling the game removes its saved data from your device.

Children

The game has no chat, no links out, no ads and no purchases, and it does not collect anything from anyone, including children. It is suitable for general audiences; the fights shown are cartoon scuffles with no blood or gore.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds online features, we will update this page and the store listing before that version is released, and describe what is collected and why.
